Calendar View — Design Options

Three distinct approaches. Choose one (or mix elements) to implement.

Option A
Grid calendar + persistent sidebar detail
Classic month grid. Today is always highlighted in blue. Colour dots summarise activity types per day. A fixed detail panel on the right shows the selected day's activities — no pop-up, always visible. Clean and information-dense.

Option B
Card-per-day grid with inline activity bars + expandable detail
Each day is a card showing a time label and coloured bars representing activity types. Clicking a day expands an inline detail row directly below that week row. More visual weight per cell — good for seeing workload at a glance without dots.

Option C
Agenda list with proportional activity bars + detail panel
Drops the grid in favour of a scrollable week-grouped list. Each day shows a proportional coloured bar split by activity type, plus a time estimate. Selecting a day populates the right-hand detail card. Best for longer study periods — no tiny cells — and very easy to scan what type of work is heaviest each day.
